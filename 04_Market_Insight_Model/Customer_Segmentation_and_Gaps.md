# Structured Market Insight Model & Brand Positioning Gaps

## Model Architecture
Built a structured market insight model to systematically evaluate customer segments and pinpoint brand positioning gaps:
1. **Input Layer:** Primary customer preference scores + secondary industry trend weights.
2. **Evaluation Scoring:** Weighted segments across *Market Size Potential*, *Unmet Need Intensity*, and *Competitor Saturation*.
3. **Output Layer:** Identification of high-opportunity segments where incumbent brand positioning fails to address core customer priorities.

## Brand Positioning Gaps Identified
* **Messaging Mismatch:** Incumbent brands emphasize legacy scale rather than solving day-to-day customer friction points.
* **Segment Neglect:** High-intent emerging cohorts lack tailored solutions designed specifically around their workflow and budget constraints.
