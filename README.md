# Market Insight & Strategic Growth Analysis
**Context:** FAC Summer Project, IITK | **Timeline:** May 2024 – July 2024

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective
This project evaluates industry trends, customer behavior, and competitor positioning to establish a clear strategic market understanding and uncover actionable growth opportunities.

## 2. Methodology & Approach
* **Secondary Research & Benchmarking:** Analyzed industry reports and market data to map macro trends, demand shifts, and competitive dynamics.
* **Structured Market Insight Model:** Designed an evaluation framework to segment target customers and pinpoint brand positioning gaps.
* **Competitor Offering Comparison:** Benchmarked key market players across features, pricing, and value propositions to identify differentiation gaps and emerging customer needs.

## 3. Key Results & Strategic Impact
* **Statistical Validation:** Evaluated target customer preferences using a primary/secondary dataset achieving a **95% confidence level**.
* **Growth Roadmap:** Translated research findings into strategic recommendations highlighting competitive advantages and high-potential growth levers.
