# Market Insight & Strategic Growth Analysis
**Context:** FAC Summer Project, IITK | **Timeline:** May 2024 – July 2024

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective
This project evaluates industry trends, customer behavior, and competitor positioning to build a clear strategic market understanding and uncover actionable growth opportunities.

## 2. Methodology & Approach
* **Secondary Research & Benchmarking:** Analyzed industry reports to map macro trends and market dynamics.
* **Statistical Dataset Validation:** Evaluated primary and secondary customer preference datasets achieving a **95% confidence level** ($Z = 1.96, E = \pm 5\%$).
* **Structured Market Insight Model:** Designed an evaluation framework to segment target customers and identify brand positioning gaps.
* **Competitor Offering Comparison:** Benchmarked competitor offerings and positioning to uncover differentiation gaps and emerging customer needs.

## 3. Key Results & Strategic Impact
* **High-Confidence Customer Insights:** Validated target customer preferences at a **95% confidence level** to guide segment prioritization.
* **Growth & Competitive Advantage:** Generated data-backed strategic recommendations highlighting differentiated brand positioning and high-potential growth levers.

## 4. Repository Structure
* `01_Business_Problem/` — Project objectives, scope, and core research questions.
* `02_Secondary_Research_and_Benchmarking/` — Industry report analysis and macro market dynamics.
* `03_Dataset_and_Statistical_Methodology/` — Primary/secondary dataset structure and **95% confidence level** validation.
* `04_Market_Insight_Model/` — Structured customer segmentation model and brand positioning gaps.
* `05_Competitor_Positioning/` — Competitor offerings comparison and differentiation gap matrix.
* `06_Strategic_Recommendations/` — Strategic decisions, growth opportunities, and competitive advantages.
