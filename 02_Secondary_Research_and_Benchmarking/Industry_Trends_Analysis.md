# Secondary Research & Industry Benchmarking

## Approach
Performed secondary research and benchmarking using industry reports to analyze macro trends and market dynamics[cite: 1].

## Key Findings
* **Market Trajectory:** Identified high-growth sub-segments outperforming traditional legacy categories.
* **Shifting Buyer Criteria:** Industry benchmarks show buyers increasingly prioritize transparent value, speed of adoption, and tailored functionality over generic brand legacy.
* **Competitive Intensity:** High saturation in mass-market tiers contrasted with under-penetrated mid-market and specialized segments.
